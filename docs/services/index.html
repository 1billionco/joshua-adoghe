How a project runs

Five stages, in this order.

The sequence matters. Skipping straight to drafting is how you get content that reads well and does nothing.

STAGE 01

Brief & audience

What the business needs to happen, who has to be persuaded, and what's already been tried.

STAGE 02

Research

Keywords, competitors, and the language your customers actually use. Sources checked, claims verified.

STAGE 03

Structure

An outline you approve before a single paragraph is written — cheaper to change here than at draft.

STAGE 04

Draft & revise

First draft, your notes, then revisions. Two rounds included as standard on every piece.

STAGE 05

Measure

What moved, what didn't, and what I'd change next cycle. Reported in plain English.
