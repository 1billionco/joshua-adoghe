The short version

How I got here.

I started out as a physicist. A BSc in Industrial Physics from the University of Benin, then a year teaching it to teenagers preparing for their WAEC and NECO exams. That background is more useful in marketing than it sounds: physics teaches you to distrust a result you can't reproduce, and teaching teaches you that if the audience didn't understand it, you didn't explain it.

I moved into writing full-time in 2017 and built from there: first as a freelance copywriter and content strategist for clients in the US, India and Nigeria, then running content for eight client brands at a Canadian design studio, then leading digital marketing for B2B clients at Just Write Creations, where I won the Tony Elumelu Foundation 2024 grant.

In 2023 I joined Tilz Collection, a UK food and drink gifting brand, as Assistant Marketing Specialist. Two promotions in two years later, I'm their SEO & Marketing Specialist. I moved to the UK in 2025 and earned my MSc in Digital Marketing at Leeds Trinity University in 2026, designing Funnel Aether along the way.

I'm based in Leeds, I have the right to work in the UK, and I work to a simple motto: excellence in all I do.
